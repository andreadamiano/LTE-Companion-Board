HIGH SIGNAL BEST PRACTISES:
    - Match impedence
    - Match differential trace lenght
    - Consider the antipad of via to match the target impedence
    - Consider the via lenght wehn matching the signal length


LPDDR4 RAM SIGNALS:
    - DQ lines (data bus, trasmitting up to 32 bits at a time, split into 2 channels A and B)
    - DQS differential lines (data strobes, validation clocks telling the ram chip when to read the data lines)
    - DM lines (data mask lines)
    - CA lines (transmit the specific memory address as well as the commansd)
    - CLK differential lines
    - CS lines (chip select, wake up a specific rank of the RAM chip to list for incoming data)
    - CKE lines (activate the internal clock circuitry)
    - reset line (wipe interal configuration registers)
    - ODT (kill echo of the high speed electric signals)
